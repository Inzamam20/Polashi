# 🎴 Unified Game Rules — Polashi & The Resistance: Avalon
### *"A Game of Wit and Deception | The Game of Hidden Loyalty"*

---

## 🧩 Overview

This is a **Social Deduction Game** for **5 to 10 players**. Players are secretly divided into two teams — a **loyal side** and a **hidden traitor side** — and must use discussion, deception, and deduction to complete or sabotage missions.

| | Polashi 🇧🇩 | Avalon ⚔️ |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme** | Battle of Plassey, 1757 | Arthurian Legend, Camelot |
| **Loyal Side** | নবাব পক্ষ (Green Team) | Loyal Servants of Arthur |
| **Traitor Side** | EIC পক্ষ (Red Team) | Minions of Mordred |
| **Missions** | Chapters | Quests |
| **Leader** | সেনাপতি | Leader |

---

## ⚔️ The Two Teams

**🟢 Team 1 — The Loyal Side**
Works together to complete missions. Most members don't know who they can fully trust.

**🔴 Team 2 — The Traitor Side**
Hidden among the group. They secretly know each other (with one exception — see Oberon / উমিচাঁদ) and work to sabotage missions from within.

> 🔒 Loyal side members do not know who the traitors are. Traitors secretly know each other — except Oberon / উমিচাঁদ, who is isolated from both sides.

## 🏆 How to Win

**🟢 Loyal Side wins** by successfully completing **3 out of 5 missions** — AND keeping the identity of their secret informant **(Merlin / মীর মদন)** hidden from the traitors.

**🔴 Traitor Side wins** by:
- Sabotaging **3 out of 5 missions**, OR
- **5 team proposals being rejected in a row** within a single mission (automatic win for that mission), OR
- If the loyal side wins 3 missions — the traitors get **one final guess** to identify the secret informant **(Merlin / মীর মদন)**. A correct guess means the traitors steal the win. One guess only.

## 🃏 Character Roles

All characters have an exact equivalent in both games. Same powers, different names and theme.

---

### 🟢 Loyal Side (Green Team / Servants of Arthur)

---

#### 👑 নবাব সিরাজউদ্দৌলা / Arthur
The leader figure and symbol of the loyal side. No special power — plays as a standard loyal member.

---

#### 🌟 মীর মদন / Merlin
The most powerful and most dangerous role in the game.

- Knows the identity of **all traitors** (except রায় দুর্লভ / Mordred, who is hidden even from this role).
- Must **subtly guide** the loyal team without revealing who they are.
- If the traitor side wins 3 missions — they lose immediately.
- If the loyal side wins 3 missions — **the traitors get one guess** to identify this player. A correct guess means the traitors win instead.
- **Play style:** Drop hints carefully. Being too obvious gets you identified and costs your team the game.

---

#### 🛡️ মোহন লাল / Percival
The protector role on the loyal side.

- During the night phase, learns who **মীর মদন / Merlin** is — but if ঘষেটি বেগম / Morgana is also in the game, sees **two** players raise their hands and doesn't know which one is the real মীর মদন / Merlin.
- Goal is to **protect মীর মদন / Merlin's identity** by creating confusion and misdirection.
- **Play style:** Shield the real মীর মদন / Merlin by sometimes acting as if you yourself might be them.

---

#### 🤍 লুৎফুন্নিসা বেগম / Loyal Servant of Arthur ①
No special power. Plays as a standard loyal member relying purely on observation and discussion.

#### 🤍 সেন্ট ফ্রে / Loyal Servant of Arthur ②
No special power. Same as above.

#### 🤍 ডেবুসি / Loyal Servant of Arthur ③
No special power. Same as above.

> These three are the equivalent of the generic **Loyal Servants of Arthur** in Avalon — honest, powerless, and relying entirely on deduction and trust.

---

### 🔴 Traitor Side (Red Team / Minions of Mordred)

---

#### 🗡️ মীর জাফর / The Assassin
The executioner of the traitor side.

- No special night power.
- If the loyal side successfully wins 3 missions, this player gets to make the **final assassination guess** — pointing to one player they believe is **মীর মদন / Merlin**.
- A correct guess means the **traitor side wins**, stealing victory at the very last moment.
- **Play style:** Watch closely throughout the game for who seems to be subtly guiding the loyal team — that is likely মীর মদন / Merlin.

---

#### 👻 রায় দুর্লভ / Mordred
The most dangerous traitor.

- **Hidden even from মীর মদন / Merlin** — the secret informant does not know who this player is.
- Knows all other traitors as normal.
- **Play style:** The hardest traitor to detect. Use this invisibility to stay trusted and influence team selection.

---

#### 🎭 ঘষেটি বেগম / Morgana
The deceiver role.

- During the night phase, **raises their hand alongside মীর মদন / Merlin**, causing মোহন লাল / Percival to see two candidates and not know which is the real one.
- Creates maximum confusion around the most important role in the game.
- **Play style:** Mimic the behavior of মীর মদন / Merlin — act like you're subtly guiding the loyal side to make মোহন লাল / Percival (and everyone else) doubt who the real one is.

---

#### 🌑 উমিচাঁদ / Oberon
The isolated wildcard.

- Does **not** reveal themselves to other traitors during the night phase.
- Other traitors also **do not know** who উমিচাঁদ / Oberon is.
- Plays completely blind — neither side fully knows this player.
- **Play style:** The most independent traitor. Must figure out who the other traitors are through gameplay, just like the loyal side does.

---

## 🌙 Night Phase (Game Setup)

Before the game begins, all players close their eyes. The following happens in order:

1. **All traitors (🔴 Red Team / Minions of Mordred)** open their eyes and silently identify each other — **except উমিচাঁদ / Oberon**, who keeps eyes closed. Then close their eyes.
2. **মীর মদন / Merlin** opens their eyes and looks around to identify the traitors — but **cannot see রায় দুর্লভ / Mordred**. Then closes their eyes.
3. **মোহন লাল / Percival** opens their eyes and sees মীর মদন / Merlin raise their hand — and also ঘষেটি বেগম / Morgana if in the game (two hands raised, unknown which is real). Then closes their eyes.
4. Everyone opens their eyes. The game begins.

## 📖 Gameplay — The 5 Missions (Chapters / Quests)

The game is played over **5 missions**. The first side to win **3 missions** wins — subject to the final guess rule.

---

### Step 1 — 🗺️ Leader (সেনাপতি) Proposes a Team

- One player is the **Leader / সেনাপতি** each round, marked by the Leader token. Rotates clockwise each mission.
- The Leader checks the **Map Card / Quest Board** to see how many players are needed for this mission (see Mission Size table below).
- The Leader nominates the required number of players and gives them a **Team Marker**, so everyone knows who has been proposed.
- The Leader **may or may not include themselves** in the proposed team.

---

### Step 2 — 🗳️ Everyone Votes

- All players **secretly select** their vote — **Thumbs Up / Approve** or **Thumbs Down / Reject** — without showing anyone.
- Everyone **simultaneously reveals** their vote.
- Only the **total count** of yes and no votes is visible — nobody knows who voted which way.

**Result:**
- ✅ **Majority approves** → The team goes on the mission. Proceed to Step 3.
- ❌ **Majority rejects** → Leader token passes to the next player clockwise. A new team is proposed for the same mission.

> ⚠️ **If 5 proposals in a row are rejected** within the same mission, the **🔴 Traitor Side automatically wins that mission!**
>
> 💡 **Tracking tip:** Rotate the current Leader's character card **90 degrees** for each rejection. Reset all cards to upright after every completed mission.

---

### Step 3 — 🎯 The Mission Happens

- Only the **approved team members** participate.
- Each receives a set of **Mission Cards** (one Success, one Fail).
- Each player secretly picks one card and passes it **face-down** to the center.
- Cards are **shuffled** and **revealed**.

**Result:**
- ✅ **All Success cards** → 🟢 Loyal Side wins the mission!
- ❌ **One or more Fail cards** → 🔴 Traitor Side sabotages the mission!

> 📌 **Rules during the mission:**
> - 🟢 Loyal side members **must always** play Success.
> - 🔴 Traitor side members **choose** — play Fail to sabotage, or Success to stay hidden.
> - **Special rule for 7+ players, 4th Mission only:** The traitor side needs **at least 2 Fail cards** to sabotage. One Fail card alone is not enough.

## 🏁 End of Game & Final Guess

**If 🔴 Traitor Side wins 3 missions** → They win immediately. Game over.

**If 🟢 Loyal Side wins 3 missions** → The game is not over yet!

The **🔴 Traitor Side (মীর জাফর / Assassin leads the discussion)** now gets their **one and only final guess** to identify **মীর মদন / Merlin**.

- Traitors may huddle and discuss quietly.
- The **মীর জাফর / Assassin** then points to exactly **one player**.
- ✅ **Correct guess** → 🔴 Traitor Side wins by assassination / betrayal!
- ❌ **Wrong guess** → 🟢 Loyal Side wins! History does not repeat itself.

## 🌊 Optional — Lady of the Lake Token (Avalon Only)

This is an **optional advanced mechanic** present in Avalon that currently has no named equivalent in Polashi. You can choose to include or exclude it.

**How it works:**
- One player starts with the **Lady of the Lake token** (given to the player to the right of the first Leader).
- After **Mission 2 is completed**, the token activates.
- At the end of each subsequent mission, the token holder **secretly learns the team alignment (Loyal or Traitor)** of one other player of their choosing — but not their specific role.
- After using it, they **pass the token** to the player they just investigated. That player uses it next round.
- A player who has already received the token **cannot be investigated again**.
- Both the token holder and the investigated player may **lie or tell the truth** about what was revealed — only they two know.

> 💡 For the app — this can be a toggleable feature. Off by default in **Polashi mode**. Optional in **Avalon mode**.

## 💡 Key Rules to Always Remember

- 🔴 Traitors know each other — except উমিচাঁদ / Oberon who is isolated from everyone.
- 🟢 মীর মদন / Merlin knows all traitors — but **cannot** see রায় দুর্লভ / Mordred.
- 🟢 মোহন লাল / Percival knows মীর মদন / Merlin — but ঘষেটি বেগম / Morgana creates a decoy.
- Voting is simultaneous — only the final count is visible, not individual votes.
- 5 consecutive rejections in one mission = automatic traitor win for that mission.
- In 7+ player games, Mission 4 needs **2 Fail cards** to be sabotaged.
- If the loyal side wins 3 missions, traitors get **one guess** at মীর মদন / Merlin — a correct guess wins them the game.
- Loyal members **must always** play Success. Traitors **choose**.
